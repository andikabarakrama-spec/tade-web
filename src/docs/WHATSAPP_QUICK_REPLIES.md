### Overview
This library provides standardized Quick Reply templates formatted for the free **WhatsApp Business App** (Business Tools > Quick Replies).
Staff and administration at TK Asy Syifa can set shortcuts (`/ppdb`, `/biaya`, etc.) in WhatsApp Business to deliver instant, polite, and professional answers to prospective parents.

### Installation Instructions for School Admins

1. Buka aplikasi **WhatsApp Business** di HP Admin Sekolah.
2. Masuk ke **Setelan Bisnis (Business Tools)** > **Balasan Cepat (Quick Replies)**.
3. Klik tombol **+** untuk menambah Balasan Cepat baru.
4. Masukkan Teks Pesan sesuai template di atas.
5. Isi **Pintasan (Shortcut)** dengan `/ppdb`, `/biaya`, `/jadwal`, dll.
6. Simpan. Saat membalas chat orang tua murid, ketik `/` di kolom percakapan untuk memilih template instan.
